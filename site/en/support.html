Support

How to get help

CF Ready is built and maintained by one person. Requests reach whoever writes the code and get an answer written by hand, not an automated reply.

Write to us

cfready@icloud.com

We answer in English or Italian. There is no phone line and no chat: email is the only channel.

Before you write

The instructions are already in the app

The Help and FAQ page inside the app, in your Shopify admin, explains what CF Ready checks, how it behaves in edge cases — foreign billing, store pickup, accelerated checkouts — and how to reopen the guided setup. The answers to the most common questions live there, updated alongside the app.

The app also gives you a link that prepares a support message with your store’s technical details already filled in. It is the fastest way to help us understand what is happening.

What to include

What we need in order to help

  • Your Shopify store domain.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • The error code, if the app showed one.
  • Roughly when the problem occurred.

Do not send us your customers’ data. We do not need Codice Fiscale values, PEC addresses, orders or addresses to understand a problem, and we would rather not receive them. Never send passwords, tokens or access keys: we will never ask for them.

Timing

When to expect an answer

We aim to reply within one or two business days. This is an operational target stated in good faith, not a contractually guaranteed service level.

If the problem is blocking your checkout, say so in the subject line: requests about stopped sales go first.

Security

Found a vulnerability?

Please do not open a public report and do not include exploitable details in an ordinary message. The procedure, response targets and the information to include are described in the SECURITY.md file in the project repository.

Alternatively, write to cfready@icloud.com stating that it is a security report, without attaching merchant or customer data.
