Shopify’s native Italian tax fields: Codice Fiscale and PEC

In checkout localized for Italy, Shopify’s native Italian tax fields are Codice Fiscale and PEC. They are separate fields with different needs and checks.

Updated 12 November 2026

Two tax fields, two decisions

Codice Fiscale uses the TAX_CREDENTIAL_IT localized field; PEC uses TAX_EMAIL_IT. They are Shopify checkout fields, separate from name, address and order notes. According to Shopify’s documentation, additional tax fields appear after the payment details and, after the order, can be found in the order details.

Shopify decides when to show them. CF Ready does not create alternative names, add boxes or reuse Address 2: it applies rules to the two fields when Shopify exposes them.

The Codice Fiscale field

An individual has a 16-character Codice Fiscale, like the sample code RSSMRA85T10A562S. Companies, public bodies and provisional codes use an 11-digit numeric code instead. CF Ready recognises both forms and checks structure, date, municipality code, omocodia and check character.

Since 6 November 2026, Shopify’s native field rejects numeric and omocodia Codice Fiscale codes on its own, before any app runs. CF Ready treats them as valid, but until Shopify fixes the field, customers with these codes cannot complete the order.

The PEC field

PEC is an Italian certified email address. An app can check its shape: a single at sign, a correctly written local part and domain, no spaces. From checkout it is not possible to know whether the mailbox exists or is really a PEC.

Four ways to manage Codice Fiscale and PEC

Codice Fiscale and PEC have independent modes:

  • Unmanaged: no errors for that field.
  • Optional and validated: empty passes, a wrongly written value is flagged.
  • Required and validated: an empty field and an invalid value block the order.
  • Required with Company (PEC only): PEC becomes required when the customer fills in Company in the billing address. If Company is empty, it stays optional and is validated when present.

Two sample setups, to adapt to what you decide with your accountant. A store that issues an invoice for every Italian order can make Codice Fiscale required and leave PEC unmanaged. A store with both consumers and businesses can keep Codice Fiscale optional and require PEC only when Company is filled in. CF Ready applies the chosen rule and does not infer the order’s tax treatment.

When the rules apply

Rules apply to deliveries in Italy and do not apply if the billing address is outside Italy, even with delivery or pickup in Italy. For store pickup, the fields that are present are checked; for mixed orders, one Italian delivery is enough. If the delivery country is missing, required fields that Shopify does not show do not block the order, unless the merchant turns on the option for orders without shipping.

No duplicated data.

One clearly labelled field reduces mistakes and keeps the data where Shopify expects it.

What an app can check

For Codice Fiscale, an app can check structure, date, municipality code, omocodia and the final character. For PEC, it can check the email address format. It cannot certify the owner’s identity or prove that a mailbox is registered as PEC.

What stays out of scope

VAT numbers, SDI recipient codes and electronic invoicing are outside CF Ready: the app does not collect them, and it does not issue, send or store invoices. For those steps you need your accounting software or your adviser.

Where the data ends up

Codice Fiscale and PEC stay in the Shopify order, where your staff and the tools that use order data read them. CF Ready does not store them. If you want a consistent format, you can turn on the option that saves Codice Fiscale in uppercase and PEC in lowercase in orders.

Why the theme is not enough

A theme field belongs to the storefront, while checkout is managed by Shopify. Besides maintenance, it can create data separated from the native tax field. Localized fields avoid that split. The pros and cons of each approach are in the comparison of ways to collect Codice Fiscale.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shopify have a native field for VAT number or SDI code?

CF Ready handles only Codice Fiscale and PEC. VAT numbers and SDI recipient codes have no equivalent native field across all Shopify plans, and CF Ready neither collects nor checks them.

Can I manage PEC without touching Codice Fiscale?

Yes. The two fields have independent modes: you can leave Codice Fiscale unmanaged and make PEC optional, required, or required only when the customer fills in Company.

What does a foreign customer enter in Shopify’s Codice Fiscale field?

With a foreign billing address CF Ready does not check the fields. Shopify’s documentation says that customers without the details required from residents can generally enter their passport number. With Italian billing and a managed Codice Fiscale, however, CF Ready accepts only a formally valid Codice Fiscale.

Does CF Ready store customers’ Codice Fiscale and PEC?

No. The check runs inside Shopify during checkout and the values stay in Shopify orders. Only if you turn on the option to standardize them in orders does CF Ready read them to rewrite them in a consistent format, without storing them.
