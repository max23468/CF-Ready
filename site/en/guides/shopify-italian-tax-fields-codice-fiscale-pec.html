Shopify checkout guide

Shopify’s native Italian tax fields

In checkout localized for Italy, Shopify provides Codice Fiscale and PEC. They are separate fields with different needs and checks.

Two fields, two decisions

Codice Fiscale uses the TAX_CREDENTIAL_IT localized field; PEC uses TAX_EMAIL_IT. CF Ready does not invent alternative names or repurpose Address 2.

Each field can be unmanaged, optional and validated, or required and validated. You can require Codice Fiscale and leave PEC optional without tying the choices together.

When rules apply

Shopify decides which fields to show. Rules apply to deliveries in Italy. They do not apply if the billing address is outside Italy. If the delivery country is missing, required fields that Shopify does not show do not block the order.

No duplicate data.

One field with a recognizable label reduces errors and keeps the data where Shopify expects it.

What an app can check

Codice Fiscale checks can cover structure, date, municipal code, omocodia and final check character. PEC can be checked for email-address form. Neither establishes the holder’s identity or proves that a mailbox is an active certified PEC.

Why the theme is not enough

A theme field belongs to the storefront, while checkout is managed by Shopify. It also risks producing a separate value from the native tax field. Localized fields avoid that split.
