How to require Codice Fiscale at Shopify checkout

Shopify already shows a Codice Fiscale field in Italian checkouts. Avoiding incomplete orders requires a rule that makes it mandatory and checks its shape before confirmation.

Updated 12 November 2026

Start with the native field

The cleanest approach does not add a theme field or rename Address 2. It uses Codice Fiscale, Shopify’s Italian localized field. According to Shopify’s documentation, additional tax fields appear in checkout after the payment details, and once the order is placed they can be found in the order details. The value stays in Shopify’s flow and customers see one clear request.

With standard settings the field stays optional: a customer can leave it empty or type a wrong value and still complete the order. That is where a rule is needed. CF Ready does not create the field or make it appear where Shopify does not show it. It works on the field that is there.

Choose the field’s mode

CF Ready gives Codice Fiscale and PEC independent modes:

  • Unmanaged: CF Ready raises no errors for that field.
  • Optional and validated: an empty field passes, an incorrectly completed one is flagged.
  • Required and validated: an empty field and an invalid value both block the order, with two separate messages.

PEC also has a fourth mode: required only when the customer fills in Company. Saving rules and activating the checkout check are separate actions, so you can prepare and test everything before any customer sees an error.

A cautious starting point.

Begin with “optional”: an empty field passes, while an incorrectly completed one is flagged. Move to “required” after checking your flow.

The steps, in order

  1. Open your store’s checkout with an Italian address and confirm that the Codice Fiscale field is visible.
  2. In the app, choose the mode for Codice Fiscale and, separately, for PEC.
  3. Save the rules.
  4. Try synthetic examples in the app’s simulator: the sample code RSSMRA85T10A562S should pass, while RSSMRA85T10A562X (wrong check character) and RSS-MRA-85T10 (hyphens and wrong length) should be flagged.
  5. Also try cases without shipping, with pickup and with a foreign billing address.
  6. Activate the checkout check and complete a test order in your own environment.

If the field label still says “(optional)”, CF Ready can update it automatically. You can also change it by hand in Shopify’s checkout text editor by following the steps shown in the app. The requirement works either way, even before you change the label.

What customers see

A wrongly typed value is flagged on the field while the customer fills in the form. An empty required field is checked before the order can be completed. If both Codice Fiscale and PEC are wrong, the customer sees both errors together. Messages can be customised in Italian and English; checkouts in other languages use English.

When the rule applies

  • Delivery outside Italy: no check.
  • Foreign billing address: no check, even with delivery or pickup in Italy.
  • Store pickup: checked when the fields are present and billing is not foreign.
  • Mixed orders: checked if at least one delivery is in Italy.
  • Required field not shown by Shopify: with a delivery in Italy it blocks the order with a general error.
  • Orders without shipping and express payments: if the delivery country is missing, only the fields that are present are checked. The “Block even when Shopify does not show the field” option, designed also for express payments that skip the form, treats an absent required field as missing when billing is Italian.
  • Technical fault, trial over or plan inactive: checkout stays open.

The store’s country and the customer’s citizenship decide nothing on their own: delivery, billing and the fields present in each checkout do.

Numeric codes and omocodia

Since 6 November 2026, Shopify’s native field rejects 11-digit numeric codes and omocodia codes on its own, before any app runs. CF Ready treats them as valid, but until Shopify fixes the field, customers with these codes cannot complete the order. Making the field required does not change this limit.

After the order

The Codice Fiscale entered stays in the Shopify order details. If you want a consistent format, you can turn on the option that saves Codice Fiscale in uppercase and PEC in lowercase in orders. It needs additional order permissions, requested only when you turn it on.

The important limit

CF Ready validates the structure of a Codice Fiscale. It does not query the Italian tax registry or certify that the code belongs to the person entering it. The merchant and their adviser decide when it must be collected.

Frequently asked questions

Can Shopify make Codice Fiscale required without an app?

Shopify shows the Codice Fiscale field in Italian checkouts, but the standard settings do not let you make it required. You need a checkout validation rule, like the one CF Ready activates on the native field.

Do I need Shopify Plus or a theme change?

No. CF Ready works on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus and uses Shopify’s own validation mechanism. It adds no theme fields, and if you uninstall, nothing is left behind in your theme.

Can I ask for it only from customers who want an invoice?

CF Ready does not know whether a customer wants an invoice, so it cannot tie the requirement to that choice. You can keep Codice Fiscale optional and validated, so anyone who enters it must enter it correctly, or make it required for every Italian order. Which one you need is for you and your accountant to decide.

What happens with express payments?

Some express payments can skip the form where Shopify shows the field. With a delivery in Italy and no foreign billing address, a missing required Codice Fiscale blocks the order with a general error. If the delivery country is missing, the absent field does not block, unless you turn on the option for orders without shipping.
