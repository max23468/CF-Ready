How to require Codice Fiscale at Shopify checkout

To require Codice Fiscale at Shopify checkout you need a rule: Shopify already shows the field in Italian checkouts, but with standard settings it stays optional. With CF Ready the field becomes mandatory and its format is checked before confirmation, so you avoid incomplete orders.

Updated 14 November 2026

Start with the native field

The cleanest approach does not add a theme field or rename Address 2. It uses Codice Fiscale, Shopify’s Italian localized field. According to Shopify’s documentation, additional tax fields appear in checkout after the payment details, and once the order is placed they can be found in the order details. The value stays in Shopify’s flow and customers see one clear request.

With standard settings the field stays optional: a customer can leave it empty or type a wrong value and still complete the order. That is where a rule is needed. CF Ready does not create the field or make it appear where Shopify does not show it. It works on the field that is there.

Choose the mode for Codice Fiscale and PEC

Rules apply to orders with delivery and billing in Italy. CF Ready gives Codice Fiscale and PEC independent modes:

  • Unmanaged: CF Ready raises no errors for that field.
  • Optional and validated: an empty field passes, an incorrectly completed one is flagged.
  • Required and validated: an empty field and an invalid value both block the order, with two separate messages.

PEC also has a fourth mode: PEC required only when the customer fills in Company. Saving rules and activating the checkout check are separate actions, so you can prepare and test everything before any customer sees an error.

A cautious starting point.

Begin with “optional”: an empty field passes, while an incorrectly completed one is flagged. Move to “required” after checking your flow.

The steps, in order

  1. Open your store’s checkout with an Italian address and confirm that the Codice Fiscale field is visible.
  2. In the app, choose the mode for Codice Fiscale and, separately, for PEC.
  3. Save the rules.
  4. In the app’s simulator, try one correct Codice Fiscale and one wrong one: the first should pass, the second should be flagged.
  5. Activate the checkout check and complete a test order in your own environment, also with the delivery and payment methods you offer.

If the label still says “(optional)”, CF Ready can update it for you.

What customers see

If Codice Fiscale is missing or wrong, the customer sees a clear message and corrects it before completing the order. If both Codice Fiscale and PEC are wrong, the customer sees both errors together. Messages can be customised in Italian and English; checkouts in other languages use English.

Some express payments can skip the form where Shopify shows the field. In the app you will find an option to decide how to handle orders where the field does not appear: test them in your store before you require the field.

Numeric codes and omocodia

Since 6 November 2026, Shopify’s native field rejects 11-digit numeric codes and omocodia codes on its own, before any app runs. Until Shopify fixes the field, customers with these codes cannot complete the order. Making the field required does not change this limit.

Where to find Codice Fiscale after the order

The Codice Fiscale entered stays in the Shopify order details: the guide on where to find Codice Fiscale in a Shopify order shows where to read it. If you want a consistent format, you can turn on the option that makes the letter case of Codice Fiscale and PEC consistent in orders.

The important limit

CF Ready handles only Codice Fiscale and PEC: no VAT number, SDI code or invoices. It runs a formal check, not an identity check: it does not query the Italian tax registry or certify that the code belongs to the person entering it. The data customers enter is not stored by CF Ready. The merchant and their adviser decide when Codice Fiscale must be collected.

Frequently asked questions

Can Shopify make Codice Fiscale required without an app?

Shopify shows the Codice Fiscale field in Italian checkouts, but the standard settings do not let you make it required. You need an app like CF Ready, which makes it required directly on the native field.

Do I need Shopify Plus to require Codice Fiscale?

No. CF Ready works on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus. It adds no theme fields, and if you uninstall, nothing is left behind in your theme.

Can I ask for Codice Fiscale only from customers who want an invoice?

Shopify checkout does not say whether the customer wants an invoice, so the requirement cannot be tied to that choice. You can keep Codice Fiscale optional, so anyone who enters it must enter it correctly, or make it required. Which one you need is for you and your accountant to decide.

How does a required Codice Fiscale work with express payments?

Some express payments can skip the form where Shopify shows the field. In the app you will find an option to decide how to handle orders where the field does not appear. Before you require the field, test the express payments you offer in your store.
