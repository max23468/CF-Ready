Codice Fiscale on Shopify orders without shipping: pickup, digital products and express payments

To ask for Codice Fiscale on Shopify orders without shipping, such as an in-store pickup, a digital service or an express payment, start from one fact: Shopify decides when checkout shows the tax fields. Here is where the field can be missing and what you can do.

Updated 14 November 2026

Who decides whether the field appears

Codice Fiscale and PEC are native tax fields of Shopify’s Italian checkout. According to Shopify’s help page they appear after the payment details, and after the order the value is in the order details. CF Ready’s rules apply to orders with delivery and billing in Italy. Without a delivery, what counts is which fields the customer sees.

Shopify decides which checkouts show them. No app can add a tax field that Shopify does not show, and a customer cannot fill in a field they cannot see. That is why it pays to test every path without shipping.

In-store pickup

With pickup chosen online, the customer does not enter a delivery address. Whether Codice Fiscale and PEC also appear on this path depends on how Shopify builds your store’s checkout. Place a test order with pickup and look at what the customer sees.

If the customer sees the field, the rules you chose apply.

Digital products and services

A digital product or a service needs no shipping, so checkout skips the delivery steps. Here too, what matters is whether Shopify shows the tax fields. If the customer sees the Codice Fiscale field, the mode you chose, optional or required, works as on shipped orders.

When Shopify does not show the Codice Fiscale field

Some express payments, such as PayPal, Shop Pay, Google Pay or Apple Pay, can skip the form where Shopify shows the tax fields. The same can happen on some orders without shipping. The customer completes the purchase without ever seeing the field.

The result is a Shopify order without a Codice Fiscale. If it happens, ask the customer for the value through the process you use for invoicing.

The option for Codice Fiscale on orders without shipping

If an order without a Codice Fiscale is a bigger problem for you than a lost order, you can choose in the app to also stop orders where Shopify doesn’t show the field. It stays off until you turn it on.

The express payment risk.

With some payment methods, such as PayPal, Shop Pay, Google Pay or Apple Pay, the order may be blocked even if the customer cannot see the field to fill in. Test these paths before turning the option on.

In-store sales with Shopify POS

Shopify POS is Shopify’s product for selling at the register. An in-store sale made with POS does not go through the online checkout, so online checkout settings do not apply to it. If a customer in your shop asks for an invoice, collect the Codice Fiscale with the process you use at the register.

How to choose

  1. List the order types in your store: shipping, pickup, digital, mixed.
  2. Try the rules you chose in the simulator.
  3. Complete test checkouts with the payment methods you offer, express ones included.
  4. Check whether the Codice Fiscale field appears in each path.
  5. Decide whether to turn the option on, knowing you can turn it off at any time.

If the field always appears, the option changes little. If it is missing in some paths, you choose between orders without a Codice Fiscale and possible blocks for customers who cannot see the field. You decide with your accountant whether and when you need the Codice Fiscale.

CF Ready’s Codice Fiscale check is formal: it verifies how the code is written, not who it belongs to.

Frequently asked questions

Does CF Ready check Codice Fiscale on in-store pickup orders?

If the customer sees the field, the rules you chose apply. Place a test order with pickup to see whether the field appears in your store.

Why did a digital order arrive without a Codice Fiscale?

Most likely Shopify did not show the field, for example because the customer used an express payment that skips the checkout form. The field may also have been optional and left empty.

What is the risk of blocking the order when Shopify does not show the field?

A customer who cannot see the field may find the order blocked without knowing what to fill in. Test the payment methods you offer before turning the option on. You can turn it off at any time.

Can I ask for the Codice Fiscale on Shopify POS sales?

Register sales made with Shopify POS do not go through the online checkout, so online checkout settings do not apply to them. In store, collect the value with the process you use for invoicing.
