Codice Fiscale on Shopify orders without shipping

CF Ready’s rules follow the delivery. When an order has no delivery country, such as a digital service or an in-store pickup, the check depends on which fields Shopify shows and on an option you choose.

Updated 12 November 2026

Why delivery matters

CF Ready applies its rules when at least one delivery is in Italy and the billing address is Italian or not yet available. In that case an empty required field blocks the order, and it does so even when Shopify does not show the field: the customer sees a general message at the top of checkout.

The rules do not apply when billing is outside Italy, even with delivery or pickup in Italy, or when every delivery is abroad. This also holds for orders without shipping.

The difficulty starts when an order has no delivery to derive a country from. Shopify may or may not show the tax fields, and CF Ready does not create them: it works on the fields the checkout exposes.

In-store pickup

With pickup chosen online and no delivery address, CF Ready checks Codice Fiscale and PEC when the fields are present at checkout and billing is not outside Italy. An empty required field blocks, and a badly written value is flagged.

In mixed orders, with part picked up and part shipped, the normal rules apply if at least one delivery is in Italy.

Digital products and services

An order without shipping follows the same logic: the rules apply if the tax fields are present and billing is not outside Italy. If the customer sees the Codice Fiscale field, the mode you chose works as in any other Italian checkout.

When Shopify does not show the field

By default, if the order has no delivery country and Shopify does not show a required field, that missing field does not block the order. CF Ready cannot ask customers for a value they have no way to enter, so it lets the order through.

The result is an order without a Codice Fiscale. This can happen, for example, with some express payments that skip the checkout form.

The option for orders without shipping

If an order without a Codice Fiscale is a bigger problem for you than a lost order, the app’s Checkout rules page has an “Orders without shipping” section with the option “Block even when Shopify does not show the field”. It stays off until you turn it on.

With the option on and an Italian billing address, a missing required field also blocks checkout when the customer tries to complete the order, with a general message at the top. It applies to digital services and in-store pickup. With billing outside Italy nothing changes: no rules apply.

The express payment risk.

With some payment methods, such as PayPal, Shop Pay, Google Pay or Apple Pay, the order may be blocked even if the customer cannot see the field to fill in. Test these paths before turning the option on.

How to choose

  1. List the order types in your store: shipping, pickup, digital, mixed.
  2. In the app’s simulator, try the scenarios without delivery, with Italian and foreign billing.
  3. Complete test checkouts with the payment methods you offer, express ones included.
  4. Check whether the Codice Fiscale field appears in each path.
  5. Decide whether to turn the option on, knowing you can turn it off at any time.

If the field always appears, the option changes little. If it is missing in some paths, you choose between orders without a Codice Fiscale and possible blocks for customers who cannot see the field. You decide with your accountant whether and when you need the Codice Fiscale.

Limits to know

CF Ready does not create missing tax fields or add them to the theme. The check is formal: it verifies how the code is written, not who it belongs to. A technical error in the app leaves checkout open. CF Ready does not act on in-store sales made with Shopify POS.

Frequently asked questions

Does CF Ready check Codice Fiscale on in-store pickup orders?

Yes, when Shopify shows the tax fields and the billing address is not outside Italy. If the same order also has a delivery in Italy, the normal rules apply.

Why did a digital order arrive without a Codice Fiscale?

Most likely Shopify did not show the field, the order had no delivery country and the option for orders without shipping was off. In that case a missing required field does not block the order.

Does the option also block orders billed outside Italy?

No. The option only applies with an Italian billing address. With billing outside Italy CF Ready applies no rules.

Does the option apply to POS?

No. CF Ready works in Shopify’s online checkout and does not act on sales made with Shopify POS.
