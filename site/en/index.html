The problem

The order arrived, the invoice didn’t

If you issue invoices for B2C orders, you need the Codice Fiscale. Shopify’s Italian checkout has a field for it, but it is optional by default: customers can leave it blank, or type it wrong. You find out afterwards, when the order is closed and you have to chase the customer by email — and hope they reply.

The usual workarounds are adding a custom field to the theme or repurposing the checkout’s “Internal” field. Both show your customer two boxes for the same value and leave theme changes for somebody to maintain.

How it works

Three steps, once

  1. Install

    The app installs from the Shopify App Store and works out on its own whether the store’s address is in Italy. It asks for no access to orders, customers or products.

  2. Choose your rules

    Decide separately for the tax code and the PEC address: ignored, optional or required. A four-step guided setup takes you through to activation.

  3. Turn it on

    From then on Shopify runs the check at every Italian checkout. No deployment, no theme edits, nothing to touch in your store.

What the customer sees

A clear message, written by you

When a value doesn’t work, the customer reads your message in the right place in the checkout — not a technical error. The texts are editable in Italian and English, and you choose whether the error appears at order confirmation or already while the customer types.

What it checks

A formal check, done properly

Counting characters isn’t enough. CF Ready verifies the code’s structure as the official formation rule defines it, and recognises the cases others get wrong.

Standard code

Sixteen characters: name positions, a coherent date of birth, the gender marker, the municipal code and the final check character.

Omocodia

Codes carrying the substitutions used when two people would otherwise generate the same tax code are recognised, not rejected.

Provisional code

Eleven digits, the form issued to those who don’t have a standard tax code yet.

Impossible dates

31 April, 30 February and the other dates that don’t exist are rejected even when the rest of the code adds up.

PEC address

A check on the address shape, including subdomains and the characters allowed in the local part.

Three modes

Each field can be ignored, optional — blank is fine, wrong isn’t — or required. The two choices are independent.

Edge cases

Whoever shouldn’t be blocked, isn’t

This is what separates a useful check from one that costs you orders.

  • Order shipping abroad: no validation.
  • Foreign billing details, even with delivery or pickup in Italy: no validation.
  • Store pickup and mixed orders: rules apply if at least one delivery is Italian.
  • Accelerated checkouts and cases where Shopify doesn’t expose the fields: no errors.
  • Unreadable configuration or a technical error: checkout stays open.
  • Expired trial or inactive plan: checkout stays open.

When in doubt, the app does not block. A lost sale costs more than an invoice completed by hand, and this is a design decision, not a fallback.

Limits

What CF Ready does not do

They sit here, with the same weight as everything else, because you should know them before installing and not after.

Not an identity check

The check is formal. We don’t verify with the Italian Revenue Agency that a code exists or belongs to whoever entered it, and a formally valid address isn’t necessarily an active PEC mailbox.

It doesn’t decide your obligations

Whether and when to collect the tax code depends on your business and your accountant. The app decides nothing on your behalf and is not a substitute for tax advice.

No electronic invoicing

The app does not issue, transmit or store invoices, and does not connect to the Italian exchange system. It handles neither VAT numbers nor SDI recipient codes.

Subscriptions and POS

Subsequent generations of recurring orders are not covered: the check runs at checkout, not on the automatic renewals that follow. In POS the app does not intervene.

Data

Your customers’ data stays in Shopify

The check runs inside Shopify, during checkout. Your customers’ tax codes and PEC addresses never reach our systems, are never logged and are never stored: the app receives the values from Shopify, evaluates them and returns only an error, if any.

We don’t read your orders, customers, products or inventory, and the permissions the app requests from Shopify stop at what it needs to work. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Before you install

Do I have to change my theme?

No. CF Ready works on the field Shopify’s Italian checkout already exposes, through the validation mechanism Shopify provides. There is no code to paste, and nothing is left behind in your theme if you uninstall.

Does it work with my Shopify plan?

Yes, on standard plans. Shopify Plus isn’t needed and no feature is reserved for one plan over another. You do need a store with an Italian address: that’s what determines whether the tax fields appear in the checkout.

Could it block legitimate orders?

It is built to avoid exactly that. Foreign orders aren’t validated, checkouts where Shopify doesn’t expose the fields raise no errors, and any uncertainty or technical error leaves the checkout open. You can also start with fields set to optional, which flags wrong values without blocking those who leave them blank.

Is the tax code really verified?

Its form is verified, not its registration. The app checks that the code is correctly built — structure, date, check character, omocodia — but it cannot know whether that code truly belongs to the customer. No checkout app can.

What happens if I stop paying?

The checkout goes back to behaving as before and no order is blocked. Your rules and messages stay saved: if you reactivate, you find everything as it was.

Who answers if something goes wrong?

The person who builds the app, by hand. There is no call centre and no chatbot: details are on the support page.
