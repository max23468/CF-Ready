How it works

From installation to checkout in three steps

  1. Install it

    From the Shopify App Store, even if the store is based outside Italy.

  2. Decide what to ask for

    Codice Fiscale has three independent modes; for PEC, you can also require it only when the customer fills in Company. Configure your rules and messages, then try them in the simulator.

  3. Activate at checkout

    Saving rules does not activate the check: you activate it when you are ready. The check applies to orders with delivery and billing in Italy.

Questions

Before you install

Do I have to touch my theme?

No, and neither do we. CF Ready works on the field the Italian checkout already has. If you uninstall one day, nothing is left behind in your theme.

Can I remove “(optional)” from the Codice Fiscale when I make it required?

Yes, even for the Codice Fiscale alone, leaving PEC as it is. CF Ready can update the label for you, or you can choose your own wording.

Does it work with my Shopify plan?

Yes, on Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus, for stores based in any country. Rules apply to orders with delivery and billing in Italy.

Can it block valid orders?

Not by default. It stops a badly written field or an empty required one. If you turn on the option for orders where Shopify doesn’t show the field, those can be stopped too. A problem in the app never blocks the checkout.

Can I require PEC only when the customer fills in Company?

Yes. PEC becomes required when the customer fills in the Company field.

Is the tax code really verified?

The check is formal, not a registry check: it spots badly written codes, but does not consult the Italian Revenue Agency or confirm that the code belongs to the customer.

Does it also handle invoices, VAT numbers or SDI?

No. CF Ready only handles the Codice Fiscale and PEC at checkout: it doesn’t issue or send invoices and doesn’t handle VAT numbers, SDI codes, subscription renewals or POS. Whether and when to collect this data is up to you and your accountant.

What if I stop paying?

The checkout goes back to how it was and no order gets stopped. Your rules and messages stay where you left them, so if you switch it back on there’s nothing to redo.

Who answers if something goes wrong?

CF Ready’s developers, directly. No call centre, no bot: it’s all on the support page.
